Why Coastal Fresh Eggs?

Why Coastal Fresh Eggs?

“Most of us buy eggs without knowing how long they have been sitting in storage. We wanted to build something simpler. Instead of buying large quantities and keeping them for days, our model is based on sourcing fresh eggs according to demand, checking them carefully, packing them and delivering them to homes. It's not about claiming that every other egg is bad. It's about giving customers another option — one built around freshness and transparency.”
Real farms. Real eggs. Real people.

About us

A Small Local Idea From Ullal.

“Coastal Fresh Eggs started with a simple thought: Why shouldn't customers know more about the eggs they buy? We wanted to build a local service that focuses on fresh sourcing, careful handling, transparent batches and convenient home delivery. We're starting small in Ullal. Instead of trying to serve everyone immediately, we'll build our customer base one household at a time.”
